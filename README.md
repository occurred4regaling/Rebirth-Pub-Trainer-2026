## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Rebirth Pub uses simple controls during its adventure sections. Begin an expedition from the available adventure interface, move through the encounter area, and use the trainer's stamina and combat options only after gameplay has started. Keep resource options disabled when returning to the pub or loading a different scene.

### Tools & Inventory

Dungeon runs provide Soul Gems and Recipes, while pub activities depend on accumulated money and management resources. Activate the corresponding resource option after the value is visible in the interface, then make a purchase, complete an action, or finish an expedition to refresh the displayed amount.

### World & Environment

The game takes place in a quiet medieval town centered on a worn-down pub. During the day, explore dangerous areas, meet NPCs, gather rare ingredients, and uncover story events. At night, operate the pub with the heroines by choosing menus, assigning tasks, and interacting with customers.

### Combat & Weapons

Adventure areas feature waves of enemies and require the player to survive until the end of the encounter. Use Unlimited Health or No Adventure Defeat for safer runs, and Unlimited Stamina when stamina limits interfere with exploration. Disable combat options before scripted scenes or minigames.
